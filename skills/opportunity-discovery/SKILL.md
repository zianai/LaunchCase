# opportunity-discovery

Translate cited problems into segment-specific hypotheses. Include alternative explanations, an existing-product alternative, assumptions and falsification experiments. Five dated reviews and two product IDs is a workflow minimum, never statistical proof. Missing evidence requires needs_more_data. Human G1 selects; this Skill cannot approve.

Inputs: frozen tenant-scoped Evidence with locator, rights, synthetic status and original hash.
Outputs: structured observations/hypotheses with evidence IDs, counter-evidence and sample limitations.
Never call paid services, mutate facts or choose human decisions. Stop on citation failure, source corruption or exhausted budget.
